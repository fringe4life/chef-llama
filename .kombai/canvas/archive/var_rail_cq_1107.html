One DOM, two templates: the rail appears when the container allows it

Named grid areas, one container query, no duplicated markup and no second component

The whole mechanism

Six grid items per lane — heading, two tags, two bars, one figure block. Narrow puts the figures underneath on their own row; wide moves figs into a right-hand column and lets the two bars and their tags stack against it. Nothing is added, removed or reordered. container-type sits on the card, because an element cannot query its own size.

/* narrow — figures underneath */
.lane{grid-template-columns:24px minmax(0,1fr);grid-template-areas:
  "head head"
  "tagN now"
  "tagP prev"
  "figs figs"}

/* tablet — the same six items, rail on the right */
@container (min-width:560px){
  .lane{grid-template-columns:24px minmax(0,1fr) 104px;grid-template-areas:
    "head head figs"
    "tagN now  figs"
    "tagP prev figs"}
}

390px — figures underneath

The figure block is the same 3-column matrix as in the wide card — heading row included — so the second figure column is never an unlabelled number. It is labelled once by row and once by column, and the previous column is muted because the heading already says which period it is. Nothing about it is hidden at this width; only its grid area differs.

The delta stays a clock hand

The needle swings between the one and five o'clock positions: above the horizontal for an increase, below for a decrease, flat for unchanged, and nothing at all when there is no earlier period to compare with — the dash in the first row. Direction is legible without reading a glyph, and it cannot be mistaken for a fifth number sitting beside four figures. The angle is coarse on purpose: three steps each way, because a needle that claims to encode an exact ratio is a lie at 14px.

What the threshold costs

At 560px the rail takes 104px and leaves the bar about 330px at a 4-bucket month, which is plenty. The threshold is a content-box width, so it is the card's padding-free width that decides — not the viewport. Two things to settle: whether 560 is the right number for a monthly hero card, and whether the week view (seven buckets) should switch at the same width, since the same rail leaves it far less bar.
