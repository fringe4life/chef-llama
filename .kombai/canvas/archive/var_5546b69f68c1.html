Kūmara, chickpea and silverbeet traybake

One tray and a 200 °C oven. The silverbeet goes in at the end, in two goes, so the stems cook through and the leaves only wilt.

Total
40 min
Prep
15 min
Per serving
620 kcal
Serves
4

8 items

1 Credit

Ingredients

  • 800 gkūmara, cut into 3 cm chunks
  • 400 gchickpeas, drained
  • 2 tbspolive oil
  • 1 tspsmoked paprika
  • 3cloves garlic, sliced
  • 1 bunchsilverbeet, stems sliced, leaves torn
  • 1lemon, zest and juice
  • 120 gfeta, crumbled
  • 1 tspflaky salt

Method

  1. 01Heat the oven to 200 °C fan and line a large tray.
  2. 02Toss the kūmara with the olive oil, smoked paprika and salt, spread it in one layer, and roast for 20 minutes until the edges darken.
  3. 03Push the kūmara to one side of the tray, add the chickpeas and garlic to the bare side, and roast for 10 minutes more.
  4. 04Fold the sliced silverbeet stems through and return the tray for 4 minutes; they should give when pressed with the back of a spoon.
  5. 05Take the tray out and fold the torn leaves through in three handfuls. The residual heat wilts them without flattening them.
  6. 06Scatter over the feta, then the lemon zest and juice. Rest 5 minutes and serve from the tray.
